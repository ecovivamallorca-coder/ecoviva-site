# EcoViva Mallorca SEO Search Roadmap

Status: working master — August 2026

## Core principle

Do not publish generic blog content for volume alone. Every search-intent page must have a clear next step into one of three EcoViva routes:

1. Technical Library — when the user is diagnosing a construction problem or researching a technical solution.
2. Intake — when the user needs pricing, a project assessment, renovation planning or a concrete proposal.
3. EcoViva process / advisory — when the user is buying, renovating from abroad, coordinating contractors or needs independent guidance.

The SEO system should operate as a feedback loop:

Research -> existing page mapping -> publish/optimise -> Search Console data -> improve -> qualified leads -> next content priority.

## Target languages

- English — international owners / buyers / expats.
- German — German-speaking owners / buyers, especially finca/villa and renovation-from-abroad intent.
- Spanish — local market and Spanish-speaking owners, with strong problem/service intent.

All important commercial and problem content should eventually exist in EN / DE / ES with native-language search phrasing rather than literal translation.

## Search Console operating method

Once impressions accumulate, prioritise pages using:

- high impressions + positions 8-20 = optimisation opportunity
- high commercial relevance + low impressions = content / indexing / keyword fit review
- strong clicks but weak enquiries = conversion / intent mismatch review
- pages already ranking in top 5 = defend and deepen rather than rewrite unnecessarily

Weekly review should record query, language, landing page, impressions, clicks, CTR, average position and lead quality when available.

## Google Ads rule

Do not begin with broad generic paid terms only. Use the organic search map to identify high-intent queries and create matching landing pages first. Candidate paid clusters:

- villa renovation Mallorca
- finca renovation Mallorca
- renovation company Mallorca English speaking
- renovation project management Mallorca
- German-speaking renovation / Bauleitung Mallorca
- roof renovation Mallorca

Problem terms can be tested later where lead quality supports it.

## Content quality rules

- Mallorca-specific, useful and technically credible.
- No mass-produced thin pages for every keyword variation.
- One strong page can target a coherent cluster of synonyms.
- Native EN / DE / ES phrasing, not literal translations.
- No unsupported technical, legal, cost or performance claims.
- Technical Library remains brand-neutral in principle where agreed and references applicable Spanish CTE / UNE standards where relevant.
- Every new page must have a defined conversion destination before publication.

## Strategic positioning

SEO should reinforce the core EcoViva proposition:

EcoViva does not simply sell labour. EcoViva provides a controlled renovation experience: independent guidance, technical understanding, coordination, quality assurance and one point of contact for property owners in Mallorca.

The objective is to enter the client's decision process earlier than a traditional contractor — at the problem, research, purchase or planning stage — and become the trusted advisor before the client starts comparing execution quotes.
