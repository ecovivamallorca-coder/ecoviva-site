ASSESS BEFORE REPLACING

Understand how the roof is failing before deciding how to rebuild it.

Leaks, damaged tiles, ageing waterproofing, poor drainage and insufficient insulation can produce similar visible symptoms while requiring very different interventions. EcoViva first looks at the existing roof, its build-up, condition, slope, drainage and connections before defining the renovation approach.

Pitched roofs

Repair or complete renewal of pitched roofs, including under-roof layers, insulation, waterproofing, ventilation and roof finishes.

Flat roofs

Renovation of flat roofs and terraces with particular attention to falls, drainage, waterproofing details and interfaces.

Traditional finishes

Solutions can retain or recreate the visual character of traditional Mallorcan curved clay-tile roofs while improving the technical build-up below.

The visible roof finish is only one part of a durable roof system.

MALLORCA CONDITIONS

Waterproofing, heat and detailing matter.

Mallorca combines strong solar exposure with periods of intense rainfall. Roof performance therefore depends on more than the tiles or membrane alone: junctions, penetrations, drainage, insulation and the relationship between the different layers all need to work together.

Waterproofing

The waterproofing strategy is selected according to roof type, pitch, existing construction and required renovation scope.

Insulation

Where appropriate, the renovation can improve thermal performance while keeping the waterproofing and roof build-up technically coherent.

Drainage & connections

Gutters, outlets, abutments, parapets, roof windows and other transitions receive specific attention because failures often begin at details.
