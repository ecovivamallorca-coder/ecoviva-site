COORDINATED INTERIORS

Different trades need to arrive in the right sequence.

Interior renovation can involve demolition, masonry, electrical and plumbing work, ceilings, floors, joinery, painting and finishing. EcoViva coordinates these elements so they form one renovation rather than a collection of unrelated jobs.

Layout & preparation

Coordinate demolition, alterations and preparation required before new finishes and installations.

Technical installations

Electrical, plumbing and other building services are coordinated with the intended layout and finishes.

Finishes & detailing

Floors, walls, ceilings, joinery and finishing details are brought together around the agreed result.

Good interior renovation is largely about managing interfaces before they become problems.

PART OF THE WHOLE PROPERTY

Interior decisions often connect to the building envelope.

Moisture, windows, insulation, terraces and exterior walls can directly influence interior finishes and comfort. When relevant, EcoViva considers these connections instead of treating the interior in isolation.
