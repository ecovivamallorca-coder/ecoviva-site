THE RIGHT FACADE APPROACH

Start with the existing wall, not with a finish catalogue.

A facade renovation needs to respond to the substrate, exposure, moisture behaviour, existing defects and desired appearance. EcoViva assesses those conditions before defining repair, insulation and finishing systems.

Repairs & rendered finishes

Repair of damaged surfaces and renewal of compatible rendered finishes as part of a coherent facade system.

External insulation

ETICS solutions can improve thermal performance while renewing and protecting the exterior facade.

Ventilated facades

Ventilated facade systems can combine insulation, a ventilated cavity and durable external cladding selected for the building.

A facade is simultaneously finish, weather protection and part of the thermal envelope.

MALLORCA CONDITIONS

Sun, rain, humidity and coastal exposure all affect facade performance.

The correct solution depends on location and construction. Cracking, staining or detached render can be symptoms of different underlying issues, while coastal properties may require particular attention to materials and fixings exposed to salt air.
