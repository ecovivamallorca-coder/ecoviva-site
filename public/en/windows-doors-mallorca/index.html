MORE THAN A FRAME

Window performance depends on the complete opening.

Replacing windows is not only a choice of material or colour. Glass specification, frame performance, shading, airtightness, ventilation and installation details around the opening all influence the final result.

Thermal & solar performance

Glazing and frame choices should respond to orientation, solar gains and the desired indoor comfort.

Installation details

Connections to walls, sills, reveals, shutters and waterproofing need to be resolved as part of the renovation.

Architecture & security

Profiles, colours, proportions and security requirements are considered together rather than as separate decisions.

The best window is only as good as its specification and installation.

MALLORCA-SPECIFIC CHOICES

Strong sun and Mediterranean architecture change the priorities.

On Mallorca, solar exposure and summer heat can be as important as winter insulation. Existing shutters, traditional openings, terraces and facade finishes also influence what is practical and visually appropriate.
