BUILDING-FIRST APPROACH

Reduce unwanted heat gain and loss before treating energy as a product list.

Comfort and energy consumption are influenced by the roof, facades, windows, shading, ventilation and technical systems together. EcoViva looks at those relationships before defining where improvements make practical sense.

Insulation

Roof and facade insulation can improve the thermal envelope when selected and detailed appropriately for the existing construction.

Windows & solar control

Glazing, frames and shading can reduce unwanted heat gain while improving comfort and usability.

Solar solutions

Solar measures can form part of a broader renovation strategy rather than being considered independently from the building.

Energy renovation works best when the building envelope and technical systems are considered together.

MALLORCA CLIMATE

Summer comfort matters as much as winter efficiency.

Mallorca's strong solar exposure means energy decisions should consider overheating, shading and cooling demand as well as insulation values. The best package depends on orientation, existing construction and how the property is used.
