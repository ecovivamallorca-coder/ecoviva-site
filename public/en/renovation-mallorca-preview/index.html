MALLORCA-SPECIFIC APPROACH

Renovating in Mallorca requires local understanding

Strong sun exposure, high summer temperatures, humidity, coastal salt air and periods of intense rainfall all influence how roofs, façades, terraces, windows and waterproofing systems perform. Many properties also combine traditional construction with later alterations. Visible damage is not always where the actual problem begins.

Moisture inside a property may originate from condensation, rising damp, a façade defect, terrace waterproofing or the roof. Cracks may be cosmetic — or indicate movement that needs further investigation.
