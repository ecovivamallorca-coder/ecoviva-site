Privacy Policy

This notice explains how EcoViva Mallorca SL handles personal data received through this website, its project-request forms and direct communications.

Privacy and data-protection requests can be sent to the email address above.

2. Data we may process

  • Direct contact data: your name and contact details, the content of an email or telephone enquiry, and related correspondence.
  • Project-request data: information you choose to provide in a request form, such as contact, property, project, budget and support-preference details, together with any other fields shown in that form.
  • Technical data: information ordinarily generated when a website is delivered, such as IP address, browser or device information, requested pages, timestamps and security logs.
  • Language preference: the website may store your selected language in your browser so that your preference can be remembered.

Please do not submit sensitive personal data unless it is necessary for your request.

3. Why we use the data

EcoViva may use personal data to answer enquiries, understand and assess a requested project or partnership, recommend an appropriate next step, prepare or discuss services, maintain business correspondence, protect the website and systems, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

EcoViva does not use website enquiry data for automated decision-making.

4. Lawful bases

Depending on the context, processing may be based on steps requested before entering into a contract, performance of a contract, EcoViva's legitimate interests in handling business enquiries and protecting its systems, compliance with legal obligations, or consent where consent is required by law.

5. Recipients and service providers

Access to personal data is limited to EcoViva personnel and service providers who need it for the purposes described above. Services used in connection with the website, enquiries and project administration may include:

  • Vercel for website deployment and hosting.
  • Fillout for embedded project-request, appointment and partner forms.
  • Google Workspace for email, calendar and business communications.
  • Holded for customer relationship management, administration and project follow-up.
  • Make for workflow automation and the transfer of information between business systems used by EcoViva.
  • Telephony and other business-service providers where necessary for direct communications and project coordination.

Service providers process personal data only to the extent necessary for the relevant services and are subject to applicable contractual and legal obligations.

6. International transfers

Some service providers may process data outside Spain or the European Economic Area. Where this constitutes an international transfer subject to data-protection law, EcoViva relies on an applicable legal transfer mechanism or safeguard required by law.

7. Retention

Personal data is kept only for as long as reasonably necessary for the relevant enquiry, project or business relationship and, afterwards, for the period required to comply with applicable legal obligations or deal with possible claims.

8. Your data-protection rights

Subject to applicable law, you may ask for access to, correction or deletion of your personal data; restriction of processing; data portability; or object to processing. Where processing relies on consent, you may withdraw it without affecting earlier lawful processing.

Send a request to info@ecoviva-mallorca.com. EcoViva may need reasonable information to verify your identity and locate the relevant data.

9. Complaints

You may raise a concern with EcoViva first. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the competent supervisory authority. In Spain, this is the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos (AEPD).

10. Security

EcoViva uses technical and organisational measures intended to protect personal data against accidental or unlawful loss, access, alteration or disclosure. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

11. Cookies, local storage and external services

The website may use essential browser storage and third-party services needed to provide requested functionality, such as embedded forms. If optional analytics or other non-essential technologies are introduced, the website and this policy will be updated and consent controls will be used where required by law.

12. Changes to this notice

EcoViva may update this notice when its website, providers or processing activities change. The latest update date is shown at the top of this page.

13. Privacy requests

For a privacy question or rights request, email info@ecoviva-mallorca.com or write to EcoViva Mallorca SL at the address in section 1.
