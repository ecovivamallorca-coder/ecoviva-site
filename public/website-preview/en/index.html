What we do

One specific project.
Or a complete renovation.

You can ask EcoViva to renovate a roof, improve a façade, add insulation, solve waterproofing problems or install solar energy. When several works belong together, we combine them in one clear renovation plan.

We assess the property, define the work, prepare one EcoViva quotation and bring in the right specialist contractors.

EcoViva renovates traditional Mallorcan homes while improving comfort, performance and everyday use. This may include a modern extension, a new façade, roof works, insulation, installations or solar energy.
