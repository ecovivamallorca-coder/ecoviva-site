A clearer way to renovate

Start with one project.
Or bring it all together.

Mallorca’s climate, existing buildings and local construction practices make renovation more complex than it first appears. Roofs, façades, insulation, waterproofing, solar and finishing details must work together—not as isolated decisions.

EcoViva first understands the property, then defines the right solution and coordinates the expertise needed to deliver it.

The same structured approach applies to one specific improvement, several connected works or a complete property renovation.

Complete renovations

Respect what belongs.
Improve what matters.

A successful renovation can preserve the character of a Mallorcan finca while introducing a contemporary extension, better comfort and carefully integrated energy systems.

This is where EcoViva’s approach becomes visible: architecture, façades, roofs, insulation, installations and finishing considered as one connected project.
