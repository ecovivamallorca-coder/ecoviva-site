VENTILATED THERMOWOOD® FAÇADE SYSTEM

VENTILATED TIMBER CLADDING WITH PIR INSULATION

SYSTEM OVERVIEW

The ventilated ThermoWood® façade combines high-performance external insulation with a ventilated timber substructure and durable thermally modified wood cladding. The open cavity supports airflow, helps drain incidental moisture and protects the external wall while creating a warm architectural finish for Mallorca's climate.

Final design must be verified for the substrate, façade height, wind exposure, fire requirements, ventilation openings, fixing method and project-specific conditions.

FIVE-PART VENTILATED TIMBER FAÇADE BUILD-UP

  1. 1

    Structural wall substrate

    Prepared masonry or concrete wall supporting the complete façade system.

  2. 2

    PIR façade insulation

    High-performance rigid insulation reducing heat transfer through the external wall.

  3. 3

    Timber battens

    Durable timber substructure creating the ventilated cavity and supporting the cladding.

  4. 4

    Ventilated cavity

    Continuous drainage and airflow space behind the external timber finish.

  5. 5

    ThermoWood® cladding

    Thermally modified timber finish providing stability and natural architectural character.

KEY TECHNICAL PRINCIPLES

✓

VENTILATION AND DRAINAGE

Keep the base-to-top cavity open for continuous airflow and drainage.

✓

MOISTURE MANAGEMENT

Use a suitable vapour-open breather membrane and correctly detailed openings.

✓

DURABLE SUBSTRUCTURE AND FIXINGS

Select exterior-grade battens and corrosion-resistant fixings for façade loads and coastal exposure.

✓

MOVEMENT AND DETAILING

Allow for timber movement; coordinate openings, corners, interfaces, base and top closures.

ASSEMBLY COMPONENTS

  • ThermoWood® cladding profile

    Thermally modified exterior timber profile.

  • Timber batten

    Substructure forming the ventilation and drainage cavity.

  • Stainless-steel screw

    Corrosion-resistant exterior façade fixing.

  • Breather membrane

    Water-resistant and vapour-open protective layer.

  • Ventilation insect mesh

    Protects cavity openings while maintaining airflow.

  • PIR insulation

    High-performance rigid thermal insulation.

NATURAL COLOUR EVOLUTION

Untreated ThermoWood® naturally develops a silver-grey surface. Appearance varies with exposure, detailing and maintenance.

IMPORTANT TECHNICAL REQUIREMENTS

Use compatible façade components selected for the substrate, insulation, cavity depth, cladding profile, fixing method, façade height, wind exposure and coastal environment. Ventilation openings, insect protection, drainage, fire performance, movement joints and all interfaces must be project-verified. Final design and execution must comply with the CTE, applicable product documentation and project-specific requirements.
