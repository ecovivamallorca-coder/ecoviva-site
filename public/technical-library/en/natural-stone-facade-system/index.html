Natural Stone Façade System

Mallorca natural stone veneers and insulated façade finish

System Overview

This façade build-up combines external thermal insulation with a premium Mallorca natural stone veneer finish. Correct substrate preparation, compatible insulation and reinforcement layers, suitable stone adhesive, mechanical support where required, and carefully detailed joints and connections help create a durable, thermally improved façade that respects Mallorca’s architectural character. Final system design must always be verified for the specific substrate, stone weight, exposure and project conditions.

Eight-Layer Natural Stone Façade Build-Up

  1. 1

    Structural wall substrate

    Assessed and prepared masonry or mineral wall supporting the complete façade system.

  2. 2

    Adhesive mortar

    Compatible mineral adhesive securely bonding the insulation to the substrate.

  3. 3

    Graphite EPS façade insulation

    High-performance external insulation helping reduce heat transfer in a compact build-up.

  4. 4

    First reinforcement / base-coat layer

    Compatible mortar forming the base for the embedded reinforcement mesh.

  5. 5

    Alkali-resistant reinforcement mesh

    Glass-fibre mesh fully embedded with correct overlaps and continuous connections.

  6. 6

    Second levelling / finishing base coat

    Levelling layer creating a stable and even substrate for the stone finish.

  7. 7

    Stone adhesive

    Flexible, compatible adhesive selected for the stone type, weight and exposure conditions.

  8. 8

    Mallorca natural stone veneer

    Selected natural stone veneer creating a durable façade finish with authentic local character.

Key Technical Principles

Substrate preparation

The structural wall must be stable, clean, sufficiently even and suitable for the complete bonded façade build-up.

Thermal continuity

Continuous external insulation reduces winter heat losses, limits summer heat gain and helps maintain more stable internal surface temperatures.

Reinforcement and load transfer

Reinforcement layers, stone adhesive, starter rails, anchors and support systems must be correctly designed for stone weight and façade conditions.

Complete system compatibility

Adhesives, insulation, reinforcement layers, profiles, waterproofing details, fixings and stone elements must form a compatible and project-verified system.

Important / Compliance

Use complete, mutually compatible components selected for the substrate, insulation, stone type, stone weight, façade height, exposure and project conditions. Mechanical support, waterproofing, movement joints, drainage, fire performance and connection details must be verified where applicable. Final execution and compliance must follow the Spanish Building Code and project-specific technical requirements.

Key Benefits

Lower heating and cooling demand

Continuous external insulation reduces winter heat loss and limits summer heat gain through the façade.

Reduced thermal bridges

A continuous insulation envelope helps limit local weak points around the structural wall.

Authentic Mallorca appearance

Natural stone veneers preserve the visual character associated with traditional Mallorcan architecture.

Protected building fabric

The external system helps protect the structural wall from temperature fluctuations and weather exposure.

Durable premium finish

Correctly selected and detailed natural stone creates a robust, long-lasting and low-maintenance façade finish.
