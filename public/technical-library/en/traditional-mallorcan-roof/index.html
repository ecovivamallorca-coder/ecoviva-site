Traditional Mallorcan Roof System

System Overview

This ventilated roof build-up combines traditional Mallorcan curved clay tiles with modern insulation, moisture control and professional detailing. The system is designed to improve thermal performance, support a healthier indoor climate and preserve the architectural character of Mallorca.

Eight-Layer Roof Build-Up

  1. 1

    Traditional Mallorcan curved clay tiles

    Traditional clay roof finish that protects the roof and preserves its local architectural character.

  2. 2

    Tile battens

    Horizontal timber battens that support and position the roof tiles.

  3. 3

    Counter battens / ventilation battens

    Vertical battens that create the drainage and ventilation cavity beneath the tile battens.

  4. 4

    Waterproofing layer

    Breathable roofing underlay or continuous waterproof membrane selected according to roof pitch and project conditions.

  5. 5

    PIR thermal insulation

    Continuous PIR insulation improves thermal performance, reduces heat transfer and helps lower heating and cooling costs.

  6. 6

    Vapour control layer

    Used where required according to the verified hygrothermal design to help control moisture migration through the roof build-up.

  7. 7

    Structural roof deck

    Timber-based structural roof deck as shown in the approved detail.

  8. 8

    Roof structure

    Timber rafters supporting the complete roof build-up.

Key Technical Principles

Roof pitch

The roof pitch determines the permitted waterproofing system and the required tile overlap.

Waterproofing selection

The waterproofing layer is selected according to roof pitch, exposure conditions and manufacturer requirements.

Ventilated roof

Continuous ventilation beneath the tiles helps remove moisture, reduces condensation risk and supports healthier indoor conditions.

Thermal performance

Continuous PIR insulation improves thermal performance, reduces heat loss and helps lower heating and cooling costs.

Installation principles

Correct detailing, compatible materials and professional installation are essential for a durable roof system.

Tile Fixing Options

Fixing is selected according to wind load, tile type and manufacturer requirements.

  1. Stainless-steel tile clip
  2. Stainless-steel fixing screw
  3. Ventilated eaves comb

Important / Compliance

The final system specification must comply with the Spanish Building Code, applicable UNE standards, manufacturer requirements and the verified project conditions.
