UNIVERSAL INSULATED FLAT ROOF SYSTEM

WARM FLAT ROOF WITH PIR INSULATION AND FLEXIBLE WATERPROOFING OPTIONS

SYSTEM OVERVIEW

The universal warm flat roof combines a structural timber deck, continuous vapour control, aluminium-faced PIR insulation and a durable waterproofing system. Different waterproofing materials can be selected according to roof geometry, exposure, detailing and project requirements.

Final design must verify structure, falls, drainage, wind uplift, vapour control, insulation, waterproofing compatibility, penetrations, perimeters and fire requirements.

SIX-PART WARM FLAT ROOF BUILD-UP

    Primary roof structure sized for the project spans and loads.

  2. 2

  3. 3

    V3 vapour barrier

    Continuous vapour-control layer below the insulation.

  4. 4

    High-performance rigid insulation forming a continuous thermal layer.

  5. 5

    Selected membrane forming the durable weather-exposed roof covering.

  6. 6

    Drainage and perimeter detailing

    Falls, drains, flashings and edge profiles direct water safely away.

KEY TECHNICAL PRINCIPLES

✓

CONTINUOUS VAPOUR CONTROL

Seal the vapour barrier at laps, penetrations and perimeter connections.

✓

CONTINUOUS PIR INSULATION

Keep the insulation layer uninterrupted to reduce thermal bridging.

✓

DESIGNED ROOF FALLS

Provide sufficient falls toward outlets without persistent ponding.

✓

COMPATIBLE WATERPROOFING

Use primers, membranes, adhesives and accessories as one system.

✓

SECURE PERIMETERS AND PENETRATIONS

Detail edges, upstands, drains, rooflights and services carefully.

✓

WIND, FIRE AND MOISTURE VERIFICATION

Verify wind uplift, fire performance, condensation and fixings.

WATERPROOFING SELECTION AND SERVICE LIFE

Performance depends on system choice, preparation, detailing, drainage, exposure and maintenance. Compatibility, joints, repairability and roof access must be reviewed.

IMPORTANT TECHNICAL REQUIREMENTS

  • Verify joist sizing, deck condition and structural capacity.
  • Provide continuous falls to correctly sized outlets.
  • Seal vapour-control laps, penetrations and perimeters.
  • Maintain continuous aluminium-faced PIR insulation.
  • Use compatible primers, membranes and accessories.
  • Secure perimeters and corners for wind uplift.
  • Detail drains, upstands, rooflights and penetrations.
  • Provide sufficient waterproofing upstand heights.
  • Coordinate edge profiles, flashings and façades.
  • Avoid trapped water and persistent ponding.
  • Verify condensation and internal humidity.
  • Confirm fire, maintenance and access requirements.
  • Allow for Mallorca sun, coast and intense rainfall.
  • Follow the CTE, product data and project requirements.
